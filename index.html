Your words belong to you

Personal, without
the profile.

No sign-up, advertising, or tracking. Your journal and forecasts are stored on your device. Add a Face ID lock if you’d like another layer of privacy.

Where is my writing stored?

Your entries are saved in protected files on your device and excluded from automatic device backups. There is no Inner Forecast account or server holding your journal.

Can I keep a backup?

Yes. Export a backup and a readable text copy from Settings. You choose where to keep them. You can import an Inner Forecast backup later, and turn on a monthly reminder to make a copy.

What about Apple Health?

Sharing check-ins with Apple Health is optional and off by default. If you enable it, those entries are subject to Apple’s Health privacy and sync settings. Your journal writing is not sent to Health.
